FAQ

The details, plainly.

What is PostPath?

A local desktop + MCP social connector from 1stStep.ai. Connect accounts once; compatible AI clients draft, schedule, and publish through your vault.

Does PostPath connect to CRMs?

Social connect and publish ship first. The next CRM layer targets HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, and Pipedrive so content can hand off into follow-up. Those CRM OAuth paths are not claimed as live yet.

Does the demo connect to my accounts?

No. It’s a browser simulation — no OAuth, no AI API call, no publish.

Which platforms?

Social today: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Google Business, Reddit, plus Snapchat and WhatsApp Channels where APIs allow. CRM next: HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive. Exact behavior follows each vendor’s APIs and your permissions.

Where are credentials kept?

Encrypted on your machine. This site never receives them.

How much does it cost?

Early access is open. We’ll charge for multi-brand, installer convenience, and team workflows — not for the right to hold your own OAuth tokens.
