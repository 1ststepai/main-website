The problem

One post.
Five apps.

Posting one idea today means five apps, five logins and five caption rewrites. Each network wants its own size, its own title and its own rules, and you are the one copying and pasting between them. PostPath puts every draft on one desk so you only do the part that needs you: saying yes.
