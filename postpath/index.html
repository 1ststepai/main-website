How it works

review, schedule.

FAQ

The details, plainly.

What is PostPath?

PostPath is a local app from 1stStep.ai. You connect networks built into the app, draft a post, review it, and schedule it on your machine.

Which networks are in the local app?

X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok (inbox handoff), Reddit, Google Business, Threads, and Pinterest are built into the local app. Real-account acceptance is not claimed.

What about Snapchat and WhatsApp?

Snapchat and WhatsApp are restricted. PostPath does not send posts to them.

Does the desk on this page use my accounts?

No. The desk is a simulated demo. It does not connect an account, call an AI API, or send a post.

Are HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, or Pipedrive included?

Those four CRMs are planned. They are not in the app. This page does not offer CRM account login.
