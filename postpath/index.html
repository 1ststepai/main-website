FAQ

The private desk, plainly.

What is PostPath?

PostPath is a private desk on your computer, from 1stStep.ai, in early access. It has not launched. It is not a scheduler, and it is not another AI writer. Your social logins stay on your machine. Every post waits for your approval before it goes out. It is built to show what posted and what failed. Live publishing is not yet verified, and that status view uses simulated platform responses for now. It does not edit video.

Does the demo connect to my accounts?

No. The private desk on this page is a simulation. It does not connect an account, call an AI, or post anything. The statuses you can preview, including what posted, what failed, rate-limited, and needs review, use simulated platform responses. Live publishing is not yet verified.

Which networks does it include?

The private desk has adapters for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Google Business, Reddit, and TikTok. Live publishing is not yet verified on any of them. TikTok is built to send to the creator inbox, not to post directly. YouTube sign-in and publishing are blocked. Snapchat, WhatsApp, and CRMs are not available.

Where are saved logins kept?

Social logins are saved encrypted on your computer, not on our servers. The saved login is an account connection. Those tokens are sent to the network you connect or post to, not to 1stStep. This page never receives them. That is local encrypted storage. It is not an audit or a compliance promise.

Is PostPath a scheduler or an AI writer?

No. PostPath is a private desk. You write the post. A reviewed post can wait until a time you pick, and that runs only while the desk is open. An assistant is optional. Cursor has a tested setup path. Claude, ChatGPT, and the other assistant logos are planned, not tested.

How do I request early access?

Use the form on this page. It opens an email to evan@1ststep.ai, and you send it yourself. Nothing is stored on this page. There is no price listed here. There is no Mac app. The Windows installer is an unsigned internal preview that has not been tested on Windows, and this page does not offer it as a download.
