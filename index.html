AN INTELLIGENT OPERATING ROUTE

Automation should make the process easier to trust.

Useful systems understand the incoming context, apply explicit rules, pause for human judgment when needed, and keep the rest of the business current.

01 / CAPTURE

Receive the work

A form, email, call, order, document, or internal request.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Gather context

Extract, classify, and reconcile the information that matters.

03 / ROUTE

Apply your rules

Prioritize, assign, draft, calculate, or prepare the next action.

04 / APPROVE

Keep judgment human

People review consequential decisions and exceptions.

05 / UPDATE

Close the loop

Write back to the CRM, notify the team, and preserve evidence.

RESPONSIBLE IMPLEMENTATION

Useful AI needs boundaries.

Human authority

People retain consequential decisions, approvals, and exceptions.

Least access

Systems receive only the data and permissions needed for the task.

Traceable work

Critical actions, evidence, and outcomes remain reviewable.

Truthful capability

Unknown stays unknown. Automation does not invent progress or proof.
