Questions

What does it cost?

Starting ranges are on this page. A Business & Bottleneck Audit is $750–$1,500 flat and is credited toward the build. A website starts at $3,500. Automations start at $2,500 per workflow. An app starts at $10,000 for an MVP. Build rescue starts with a $1,000 code review. Consulting is $150–$200/hr, or a retainer from $1,500/mo. Every project is scoped on a free call.

How long does it take?

The timeline follows the scope. A focused site or one workflow is shorter than an app or a rescue. The free call is where the timeline gets named.

I already started a build, can you finish it?

Yes. Build Rescue & Handoff starts with a $1,000 code review, then a plan to finish what a vibe coder or you started.

I don't know what I need, where do I start?

Begin Your Journey is six questions in the browser, and it does not create an account. Or book the free 20-min call. A paid audit is the deeper first pass, and it is credited toward the build.

Do you work with my tools?

Yes. The build connects the website, CRM, calendar, and the other tools you already run. The call is where that stack gets named.
