# 1stStep.ai logo — locked brand decision

The existing logo used by the current live `https://www.1ststep.ai/` website is the canonical 1stStep.ai brand mark. This is a user-locked decision for the parent site, `/os`, 1stStep OS, Audit, Job Agent, and all marketing, social, video, presentation, diagram, launch, and press assets. Build the visual system around the existing logo. Do not redesign, reinterpret, redraw, recolor, crop, replace, or generate the logo with AI. Signal Path is permitted only as a secondary diagram and motion language.

## Placement rules for new creative

- Use the canonical transparent asset as a single, intact image. Preserve its native 1147:946 aspect ratio (`width: auto; height: auto; object-fit: contain` as applicable). Do not stretch, crop, mask, rotate, filter, retype, or separate its symbol, wordmark, or tagline.
- Keep a clear area around the displayed artwork of at least **15% of the displayed logo height** on every side. No copy, diagram line, photograph edge, glow, or other mark enters this area. This is a conservative working safe area, not a claim of an older formal brand standard.
- For marketing artwork where the full lockup and tagline must be read, use at least **360 CSS pixels wide** on screen or **90 mm wide** in print, subject to final visual legibility review at actual output size. If the placement is smaller, restructure the layout rather than crop or recreate the logo.
- Place it over a quiet, high-contrast field. The repo's dark-square exports are available when an asset needs a built-in dark background. Test the transparent asset on the actual background; do not assume a light-mode variant exists.
- Small navigation UI may pair the intact logo image with separate accessible `1stStep.ai` text, as the site already does. Preserve the image ratio even when it is rendered small; do not rely on its tiny tagline for identification.
- Keep product names such as “1stStep OS” and “1stStep Audit” as surrounding text. They do not get replacement marks made from the Signal Path motif.

Current `/os` palette around the locked mark: `#070912` near-black navy, `#101525` and `#151b2d` surfaces, `#f7f8ff` off-white, `#79a7ff` active blue, `#73def7` cyan, and `#a99cff` violet. Space Grotesk is display, Inter is UI/body, and DM Mono is used for system labels. These supporting tokens may evolve deliberately; the logo artwork may not.
