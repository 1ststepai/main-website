# 1stStep Handoff Standard (Draft)

Status: internal Release 1 draft. This is a delivery checklist, not a promise that every item applies to every engagement.

## 1. Scope and authority

- Record the agreed outcome, exclusions, acceptance criteria, decision owner, and release authority.
- Identify third-party terms, credentials, billing, and actions that remain client-owned.
- Keep assumptions separate from verified facts.

## 2. Ownership and access

- Record the owner for source control, domains, hosting, databases, analytics, email delivery, and other required services.
- Transfer or verify client-controlled access without placing secrets in documentation or chat.
- Document recovery paths and emergency contacts where applicable.

## 3. Release evidence

- Identify the exact release SHA and deployment environment.
- Record relevant tests, results, known limitations, rollback point, and remaining production checks.
- Distinguish local, fixture, Preview, hosted, and Production evidence.

## 4. Operating material

- Provide the minimum runbook for common operations, failures, backups, and support.
- Document data flows, consent boundaries, external dependencies, and manual gates.
- List open work with an owner and next action.

## 5. Acceptance

- Walk through the critical user journey with the client or designated owner.
- Confirm access and artifacts are usable by the intended owner.
- Record acceptance, exceptions, and any time-bounded support terms.

This standard does not guarantee commercial results, uninterrupted third-party services, inbox delivery, or outcomes outside the agreed scope.
