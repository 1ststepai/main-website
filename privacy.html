Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect information you submit through forms, booking pages, email, calls, or messages, including your name, company name, email address, phone number, business details, and project goals.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To understand project goals and recommend an appropriate website, product, or first-version scope.
  • To deliver services, onboarding, reporting, support, and follow-up communications.
  • To send occasional marketing emails only when you separately choose to receive them.
  • To improve our website, offers, and client experience.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, schedule appointments, communicate with prospective clients, process data, or deliver client work.

Cookies And Tracking

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand traffic and measure actions such as portfolio visits, fit-check requests, local-offer applications, journey starts and diagnosis views, and confirmed bookings. Our analytics events do not include the email address, website URL, or other personal details entered into forms.

The Begin Your Journey diagnosis runs in your browser and is saved in that browser until you clear it or clear browser data. Its answers are not submitted to 1stStep.ai unless you explicitly send a review request or choose to send a prefilled email from your mail application. A submitted review request includes your email address and diagnosis, is encrypted in our operational storage, and expires after 90 days. The request does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

For the 1stStep OS public first look, you enter your email and consent before your public website or GitHub link. When you submit the link, we save your email address, website origin or public GitHub repository link, first-look consent, optional marketing choice (and its wording version if selected), request reference, and time in encrypted operational storage before scanning. If saving fails, the scan does not run. We fetch public webpage or GitHub metadata to show observations and the free roast in your browser. We do not save the scanned page path, query string, or result in this lead record. The record expires automatically after 90 days. The separate, unchecked marketing choice is optional; leaving it unchecked does not affect the first look. No report email is sent, and this form does not currently add anyone to a marketing list or send campaigns.

The separate OS setup preview keeps your goal and project answers in your browser until you choose to submit a setup inquiry. If you submit it, we store your email, goal, four project-state answers, any public link, consent, request reference, and time in encrypted operational storage for 90 days so we can follow up about a possible setup. This does not create an account, connect code, start an audit or OS, charge you, or subscribe you to marketing. If storage is unavailable, the page does not claim to have received your request; you may instead review and send an email draft from your own mail application.

If you arrive through a campaign link, attribution parameters such as UTM values and advertising click identifiers may be stored in your browser and passed to our booking provider so we can understand which campaign led to an inquiry or booking.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request that we update, correct, or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to business, legal, and operational requirements. You may opt out of optional marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe method in the message or by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact 1stStep.ai through the booking or contact method provided on this website.
