Using AI for Business: What to Keep Private and How to Check Answers

By Evan Pancis · 1stStep.ai · November 11, 2026

Short answer: AI output is a draft, not proof. Practice with fictional or public information, check your company rules and your AI provider's current, plan-specific data controls before sharing business information, and verify every price, date, policy and citation against your own source of truth.

Examples in this article are fictional and written for teaching.

What business information should I keep out of AI tools?

Start by deciding what kind of information you have. If you're unsure, use fictional details while you ask the person responsible for your company's data.

A simple information check before you paste
InformationExamplesStarting rule
PublicWebsite copy, published hoursGenerally fine for practice; check that it is meant to be public.
Fictional / practiceMade-up business factsIdeal for learning; keep it clearly labeled fictional.
InternalPricing drafts, business plansOnly per company policy and your plan's settings.
SensitiveCustomer personal data, health or financial details, passwords, contractsKeep out unless your organization has approved a tool for that information.

Removing names does not always anonymize a record. A location, unusual job, appointment time or combination of details may still identify someone. For beginner practice, replace the whole scenario with a fictional one.

Does turning off model training make my data private?

No single toggle answers that question. Training concerns model improvement; history concerns saved chats; retention concerns storage duration; access concerns who can view information. Check each separately.

According to OpenAI's Data controls in ChatGPT:

  • Controls vary with sign-in status, plan and workspace settings.
  • Personal Free, Plus and Pro workspaces share data for model improvement by default. To opt out, go to Settings → Data controls → “Improve the model for everyone” and switch it off.
  • That change leaves saved chats in place; deletion is separate.
  • Temporary chats are excluded from model improvement but may remain stored for safety for up to 30 days.
  • By default, ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu workspace content, and API inputs and outputs, are excluded from training.
  • If you submit thumbs up/down feedback, the associated conversation may be used for training.

Checked November 11, 2026; settings change — recheck before relying on them.

Using another assistant? Check your tool's current page: Anthropic's model-training privacy help or Google's Gemini privacy help. These settings do not establish that a tool is safe or compliant for your business. This article is not legal advice.

Does AI tell the truth?

Not reliably enough to skip review. OpenAI's Does ChatGPT tell the truth? explains that answers can be incorrect or misleading while sounding confident. Errors can include wrong facts or dates and invented quotes or citations. Its advice is to treat the response as a first draft and verify important information.

How do I check an AI answer before I use it?

Go back to the record that actually determines the answer. A polished sentence is not evidence.

Match the claim to its source of truth
Claim typeWhere to checkIf it doesn't match
PriceYour approved current price listCorrect it; don't estimate a missing price.
Date / hoursYour calendar and published hoursCorrect the date, hours and time zone.
Policy / refundYour current written policyRemove unsupported wording; ask the policy owner.
Citation / statisticOpen the source link and find the actual supporting passageRemove it if the source is missing, irrelevant or does not support the claim.
Legal / taxAn appropriately qualified professionalStop and get advice before acting.

What does a fictional policy mistake look like?

This illustrative example was written for this article. It is not a recorded tool run.

Fictional fact sheet

  • Business: Maple Street Dog Grooming, Morris County, NJ — fictional.
  • Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; appointments required.
  • Services: dog bathing, brushing and nail trims.
  • Not known: prices, cancellation fees, cancellation policy or refund policy.

Fictional draft — incorrect: “Full refund within 30 days.”

Review: The fact sheet has no refund policy. Flag the sentence as unsupported, remove it from the customer-facing draft and write “Needs confirmation” in your working notes. Ask the business owner for the approved policy before writing a replacement. Do not infer a refund promise from what another business offers.

When should I stop and get help?

Stop before sharing regulated data or contracts, relying on legal, medical or financial advice, or connecting anything that sends automatically to customers. Ask your data owner, qualified advisor or the person responsible for that system to review the task and approve the approach.

If you can't check a claim or find who owns the decision, leave it out and ask. “Needs confirmation” is a useful working note, not permission to publish.

What should I check before I paste and before I use?

Before you paste

  • Can I use fictional or public details instead?
  • Have I checked company rules and the current settings for this exact plan and workspace?
  • Have I removed private details, including combinations that could identify someone?
  • If the information needs approval, have I obtained it?

Before you use

  • Does every factual claim match an authoritative record?
  • Have I checked prices, dates, policies and each source link?
  • Have I removed unsupported promises and marked missing details in my notes?
  • Has a person decided what to approve, edit or reject?
  • Is automatic sending off?

Ready to practice the whole process? Follow your first business AI task, prompt and review using fictional facts.

Frequently asked questions

Does turning off training delete my chats?

No. OpenAI’s Data controls in ChatGPT page says switching off model improvement does not delete saved chats. Delete chats separately if you want to remove them; check the current help page and your workspace rules.

Can AI sources be wrong?

Yes. A source may be invented, outdated or unrelated to the claim. Open the link, find the original passage and check that it supports the exact statement. If you cannot verify it, leave the claim out.

Can anonymized details still identify someone?

Yes. Removing a name may leave enough clues to identify a person, such as their location, job or appointment time. Use a fully fictional scenario for practice instead of lightly editing a real customer record.
