A useful draft still needs a check.

Illustrative lesson example—not a student result.

Meet Oak Street Repairs, a fictional business created for this example.

01 / SERVICE FACTS

  • Repairs by appointment.
  • Service area: Morris Plains.
  • No emergency service is listed.

02 / AI DRAFT / CHECK

“We offer 24/7 emergency repairs.”

The check: That promise is not in the facts. A confident sentence is not evidence.

03 / REVIEWED ANSWER

“We offer repairs by appointment in Morris Plains. Contact us to discuss availability.”

That is the habit we want you to practice: give clear context, inspect the draft and revise before using it. Use fictional or approved public information for practice, not private client data.
