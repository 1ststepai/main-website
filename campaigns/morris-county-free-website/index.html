Questions before you apply.

The important boundaries are here in plain language. Exact project terms are confirmed with selected businesses in writing.

Why is the website build free?

This is a limited local portfolio initiative. It lets 1stStep.ai invest directly in ten Morris County businesses while building more visible local website work and relationships.

Does 1stStep.ai buy my domain or hosting?

No. 1stStep.ai does not purchase domains, hosting, email, plugins, or other paid services for this offer. You connect a domain you already own or purchase it directly in your own account. If a provider requires a paid plan, the business approves and pays that provider directly. No client expense is incurred by 1stStep.ai.

Can I apply without a current website?

Yes. A current website is optional. The business must still be active, locally based, and ready to provide accurate content and feedback.

How are the ten businesses selected?

Applications are reviewed in received order. The first ten that meet the Morris County, authority, readiness, scope, and portfolio requirements may be accepted. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance.
