What 1stStep.ai does

The core idea is simple: maximize what the owner already has, save time, and help the business grow. Most of the businesses we talk to don't need another subscription. They need the tools they already pay for to work together, so leads get answered, the right person is notified, and nothing depends on someone remembering.

In practice that means four kinds of work:

  1. Websites that get leads. A clear offer, proof, one main next step, and forms that send every inquiry to a person. See Business website that gets leads.
  2. Workflow automations, mostly in GoHighLevel. Lead capture, routing, instant acknowledgement, missed-call text back, reminders and follow-up sequences, built inside the account the business already has. See Automation for lead capture and follow-up.
  3. Integrations and apps. Connecting the website, CRM, calendar and other tools, or building an internal tool or a first version (MVP) of an app when off-the-shelf software doesn't fit. See Internal tools and Build an MVP.
  4. Teaching owners to use AI. Practical help using AI on the bottleneck the business has today, with a person reviewing anything that goes to a customer.

Who it's for

  • Insurance agencies that get quote requests from forms, phones, carriers and referrals and want every one answered and routed. See Automation for insurance agencies.
  • Real estate brokerages that get inquiries from portals, IDX search, signs and open houses and want each lead to reach the right agent quickly. See Automation for real estate brokerages.
  • New business owners who want a website and a simple follow-up setup without buying tools they don't need yet.

How a project starts

  1. Free Bottleneck Call (30 minutes). We talk through where time or leads are slipping and whether 1stStep.ai is a fit. It's a conversation, not a systems audit. Book at https://www.1ststep.ai/book/.
  2. Optional Business & Bottleneck Audit. A paid, flat-fee look at how leads and work actually move through the business, with a written plan. The fee is credited toward the build. To request a System Audit directly, email evan@1ststep.ai.
  3. Build. Scoped work on the tools you already use, tested end to end, with a person kept in the loop for customer conversations.

What 1stStep.ai is not

  • Not a job-search tool. 1stStep.ai also makes a separate product, the 1stStep Job Agent at https://app.1ststep.ai/, for job seekers. The consultancy described on this page is the main business at https://www.1ststep.ai/.
  • Not the same company as firststep.ai or other "First Step" businesses. 1stStep.ai is spelled with a "1" and is based in Morris Plains, NJ.
  • Not a guarantee. 1stStep.ai does not guarantee revenue, rankings, leads or results.
  • Not generic consulting. Work is scoped around a specific bottleneck, such as slow lead follow-up or disconnected tools.
  • Not a GoHighLevel reseller requirement. If you already have a CRM that works, we build on it.
