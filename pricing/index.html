Pricing questions

What do 1stStep.ai services cost?

Published starting ranges: Business & Bottleneck Audit $750–$1,500 flat, credited toward the build. Website build from $3,500. Automations & integrations from $2,500 per workflow. App development from $10,000 for an MVP. Build rescue & handoff starts with a $1,000 code review. Consulting & support $200/hr, or retainers from $1,500/mo.

Why are these starting ranges and not fixed prices?

The final price depends on scope: how many pages, lead sources, tools, people and integrations are involved. Every project is scoped on a Free Bottleneck Call (30 minutes) before any proposal.

Is the Free Bottleneck Call the same as the Business & Bottleneck Audit?

No. The Free Bottleneck Call is a free 30-minute conversation. The Business & Bottleneck Audit is a paid, flat-fee review of how leads and work move through the business, and its fee is credited toward the build. System Audit requests can be sent to evan@1ststep.ai.

Do I have to buy new software?

Usually not. 1stStep.ai builds on the tools a business already uses, most often GoHighLevel, plus its website, calendar, phone and CRM.

Are results guaranteed?

No. 1stStep.ai does not guarantee revenue, rankings or results.
