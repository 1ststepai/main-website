## Smithery

PostPath is a local social drafting desk. An AI client can save a draft for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and the other connected networks. The person approves it on their computer before anything is sent. Account logins stay in the local vault.

## Cursor MCP directory

PostPath drafts social posts on your computer and waits for your approval. Connect with the MCP URL from the PostPath desk, or with the stdio command `postpath serve`.

## Claude connectors

PostPath lets a connector save social drafts and list the approval queue. Publishing requires the queue:approve scope, which the consent screen leaves off until you tick it. Instagram posts are public. YouTube on the 1ststep-ai profile needs a vidIQ check first.
