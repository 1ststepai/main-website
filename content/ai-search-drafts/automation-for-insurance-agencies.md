**Short answer:** An insurance agency should automate the steps around the conversation, not the conversation itself. That means an instant, honest reply to every quote request, missed-call text back, routing each lead to the right producer or CSR, a reminder when nobody has followed up, and renewal and review-request reminders. Most of this can be built in GoHighLevel and the phone system an agency already has. Licensed staff should still handle quotes, coverage and advice. 1stStep.ai builds these workflows for insurance agencies; it starts with a Free Bottleneck Call (30 minutes).

## Where agencies usually lose time and leads

- **Quote requests from many places.** Website forms, carrier and comparison-site leads, referrals, phone calls and walk-ins each land somewhere different.
- **Missed calls.** A shopper who reaches voicemail often calls the next agency.
- **Producer vs. service confusion.** New-business quotes, claims questions and service requests all arrive in the same inbox.
- **Renewals and cross-sell handled from memory.** Reminders live in someone's calendar, not in the system.
- **Retyping.** The same details get entered into the website form, the CRM and the agency management system.

## What to automate first

1. **Instant acknowledgement for every quote request.** A short text or email: we got your request about {line}, {name} will contact you {realistic time}. No prices, no coverage statements.
2. **Missed-call text back.** When a call goes unanswered, text the caller from the agency number with a simple next step and opt-out language.
3. **Routing rules.** New business to producers by line or territory; service and claims questions to the right CSR. Write the rule down so the team can explain it.
4. **No-contact escalation.** If a lead hasn't been touched within your target time, notify the assigned person and a backup.
5. **Follow-up sequence that stops on reply or bind.** A few touches over the first couple of weeks, ending as soon as the lead responds, books or binds.
6. **Renewal and review reminders.** Reminders built from verified policy dates, and a review request after a policy is bound, sent only with consent.

## What to keep human

- Quotes, coverage explanations and recommendations
- Anything that requires a license or could be read as advice
- Claims conversations
- Final review of any AI-drafted message before it goes to a client

## Compliance notes

Texting and calling consumers is regulated. Confirm consent wording, opt-out handling, calling hours and record-keeping with your compliance contact. The FCC's consumer guide is a starting point: https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/stop-unwanted-robocalls-and-texts. Business texting through GoHighLevel generally requires A2P 10DLC registration.

## Common questions

### What should an insurance agency automate first?
Instant acknowledgement of every quote request, missed-call text back, routing to the right producer or CSR, and a reminder when nobody has followed up.

### Can an insurance agency automate follow-up without buying new software?
Usually yes. Most agencies can build these steps in a CRM they already pay for, such as GoHighLevel, plus their existing phone system.

### Should automated messages quote prices or coverage?
No. Automated messages should acknowledge and schedule. Quotes, coverage and advice should come from licensed staff.
