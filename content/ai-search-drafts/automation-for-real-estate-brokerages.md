**Short answer:** A brokerage should automate the first reply, the routing and the reminders, and leave the conversation to agents. Send every portal, IDX, sign-call and open-house lead into one CRM, reply instantly and honestly, route it to an agent by a written rule, and escalate to a backup if nobody responds in time. This can usually be built in GoHighLevel or the CRM the brokerage already pays for. 1stStep.ai builds these workflows for brokerages, starting with a Free Bottleneck Call (30 minutes).

## Where brokerages usually lose leads

- **Too many inboxes.** Portal leads, IDX registrations, website forms, sign calls and texts land in different places.
- **Unclear ownership.** Round-robin rules exist in someone's head, not in the system.
- **Nights and weekends.** Leads arrive when agents are showing homes or off.
- **Open-house sign-ins** that never get entered.
- **No visibility.** The broker can't easily see which leads were never contacted.

## What to automate first

1. **One lead list.** Connect each source so a new lead creates or updates a contact in the CRM with source, property or area of interest, and contact preference.
2. **Instant, honest acknowledgement.** "Thanks for asking about {address}. {Agent} will reach out {realistic time}." No availability or pricing promises.
3. **Routing by a written rule.** Listing agent for listing inquiries, round robin or area-based for the rest, and a fallback when the rule can't decide.
4. **No-contact escalation.** If the assigned agent hasn't responded within your target, alert a backup or the broker.
5. **Missed-call text back** for the office and sign numbers.
6. **Open-house follow-up.** A sign-in form that feeds the CRM and starts a short follow-up, with consent.
7. **Stop on reply.** Every sequence ends when the person responds or books.

## Using AI day to day

Agents and staff can use AI to draft listing descriptions for review, summarize showing feedback, turn call notes into CRM notes, and prepare client update emails. A person should review anything that goes to a client, and listing copy should be checked for fair housing compliance. 1stStep.ai teaches brokers and agents to use AI on the bottleneck they have now.

## What to keep human

- Pricing opinions, negotiations and contract questions
- Anything a licensee must handle under your state's rules
- Final review of AI-drafted client messages and listing copy

## Compliance notes

Confirm texting consent, opt-out handling and calling rules with your broker of record or compliance contact. The FCC's consumer guide is a starting point: https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/stop-unwanted-robocalls-and-texts.
