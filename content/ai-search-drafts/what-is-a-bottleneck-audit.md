## What a "bottleneck" usually looks like

A bottleneck is the step where work piles up. In small businesses it's rarely the software itself. It's usually one of these:

- **Leads with no owner.** A web form emails a shared inbox and everyone assumes someone else answered.
- **Missed calls that go nowhere.** The caller hits voicemail and never hears back.
- **Copy-and-paste between tools.** Someone retypes the same details from a form into the CRM, then into a quote tool or spreadsheet.
- **Follow-up that depends on memory.** There's no reminder if a quote or showing request sits for a day.
- **Tools that don't talk to each other.** The website, calendar, CRM and phone system each hold part of the story.
- **The owner as the bottleneck.** Every decision or approval waits for one person.

## What the audit covers

A good audit looks at the real path, not just the tool list. The 1stStep.ai Business & Bottleneck Audit covers:

1. **Every way a lead or request reaches you:** forms, phones, texts, portals, referrals, walk-ins.
2. **Where each one lands today** and who is responsible for it.
3. **The tools in the path:** website, CRM (often GoHighLevel), calendar, phone, email, spreadsheets.
4. **The handoffs:** where information is retyped, waits for approval, or gets lost.
5. **What to fix first:** a short, prioritized list, ranked by how much time or how many leads each fix is likely to save, and whether each one can be done with tools you already have.

## What happens after the audit

You get the prioritized list and decide. You can build the fixes yourself, hire someone else, or have 1stStep.ai build them, in which case the audit fee is credited toward that work. Typical next builds are lead follow-up automations, a website that routes inquiries properly, or a small integration between tools you already pay for.

## Questions to ask any audit provider

- What exactly will I receive at the end, and in what format?
- Will you look at my real lead path and tools, or only interview me?
- Do you recommend new software, or start with what I already have?
- Is the audit fee credited if I hire you to build?
- What happens if the audit finds nothing worth fixing?
